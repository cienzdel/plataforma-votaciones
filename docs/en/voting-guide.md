# Voting guide

> 🚧 **Draft.** Describes the **intended** behaviour; the platform is not implemented yet. Screenshots will come with v1 and will use fictitious data.

## Before the session

- You need a **phone or computer with an up-to-date browser** (recent Chrome, Firefox, Safari or Edge) and access to your e-mail. If your browser is too old, the site will tell you: use another device or tell the officials.
- If you cannot attend, you can **delegate your vote** to another member through the procedure stated in the meeting notice. You will get an e-mail when the officials register it.

## Signing in

1. Open the platform link (sent by e-mail or projected in the room).
2. Enter your e-mail address. You will receive a **single-use code** valid for 10 minutes.
3. Enter it. Your session lasts 12 hours.

If you cannot reach your e-mail in the room, ask the officials for **assisted access**: they check your identity in person and give you a code. You will get an e-mail telling you this happened.

## Voting

1. **Attendance:** the officials mark you present (also if you attend by video call). You can only vote if you are present when the vote opens.
2. **Opening:** when the officials open a vote, your browser automatically requests your anonymous credential. **Keep the page open.**
3. **Ballot:** pick your options. If you hold proxy votes, you will see one ballot per vote ("Your vote", "Proxy vote of…") and you can vote differently on each.
4. **Review:** before sending you will see a summary. A vote that has been sent **cannot be changed**.
5. **Receipt:** once sent, it is stored in your browser and you can download it.

### Ballot options

- **For**, **against** and **blank** count as votes cast.
- **Abstain** does not count as cast. It still uses your credential and gives you a receipt.
- Elections to positions also offer **"against all"**.
- In committee-post votes, each cell is **abstain** unless you change it.
- In limited votes, an unmarked ballot counts as **blank**.

## Afterwards

- When each vote closes you will receive an **e-mail** with the result's cryptographic digest and the **number of credentials issued in your name**. If it does not match the ballots you received (yours plus your proxies), **tell the officials**.
- Open the platform again in the **same browser**: it automatically checks that your vote is on the board.
- More in the [verification guide](verification-guide.md).

## Take care

- **Do not show your receipt to anyone:** it reveals how you voted.
- **Do not clear browser data** while a vote is open: you would lose your credential and your vote.
- Vote where nobody can see your screen.
